## 4. Qualitative Deep Dive: 4 Key Company Questions

### Q1: How does the company make money?
SELLAS Life Sciences is a development-stage biopharmaceutical company. It does not currently generate commercial product sales. Its business model centers on developing high-value oncology therapeutics through pivotal clinical proof-of-concept, at which point the company monetizes assets through out-licensing agreements, regional commercial rights partnerships, upfront technology access fees, and developmental/commercial milestones. Long-term profitability is contingent upon commercial approval and sale of Galinpepimut-S and SLS009.

### Q2: Are its products or services in demand, and why?
Yes, clinical demand is exceptionally high. Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) is an aggressive, deadly hematologic cancer. Patients who achieve second complete remission (CR2) face an extremely grim prognosis, with historical median overall survival of only 5.4 months under Best Available Treatment. Lead drug candidate **Galinpepimut-S (GPS)** is an engineered peptide immunotherapy targeting the **Wilms Tumor 1 (WT1)** oncoprotein, which is overexpressed in over 90% of AML cases. GPS trains the patient's immune system to destroy residual leukemic cells, potentially transforming AML into a manageable chronic disease. The FDA has awarded GPS **Fast Track** and **Orphan Drug** designations.

### Q3: How has the company performed in the past?
SELLAS has historically struggled with public market volatility, reverse stock splits, and equity dilution—a trajectory familiar to small-cap clinical biotech investors. However, from an operational and clinical perspective, management has demonstrated remarkable resilience by successfully completing patient enrollment in the pivotal Phase 3 REGAL trial despite acute macro and supply chain headwinds.

### Q4: Is the company positioned for growth and profitability?
The company is positioned at an asymmetric clinical inflection point. If the Phase 3 REGAL trial reaches its primary overall survival endpoint with statistical significance, SELLAS will be positioned to file for FDA Biologics License Application (BLA) approval and secure major commercial co-development partnerships with top-tier global pharmaceutical companies.
