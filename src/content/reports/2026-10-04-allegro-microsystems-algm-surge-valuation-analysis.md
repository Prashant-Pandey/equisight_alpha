### Core Qualitative Examination: 4 Key Company Questions

#### Q1: How does the company make money?
Allegro MicroSystems designs, manufactures, and markets high-performance sensor ICs (principally Hall-effect and xMR magnetic sensors) and power ICs. Top-line revenue ($3.71B TTM) is generated through volume component deliveries to global automotive Tier-1 suppliers, industrial automation OEMs, and clean energy infrastructure providers, supplemented by specialized software licenses and engineering support.

#### Q2: Are its products or services in demand, and why?
Yes. Demand is structurally driven by the electrification of mobility (EV traction motor control, advanced driver assistance systems [ADAS], electronic steering) and high-efficiency industrial robotics. Magnetic sensors provide precise contactless speed, position, and current measurement essential for automated systems.

#### Q3: How has the company performed in the past?
Historical performance reflects a cyclical semiconductor trajectory. Gross margins have remained steady at 47.2%, demonstrating pricing discipline. While cyclical inventory destocking temporarily reduced net income to $0.07B, cash flow generation remained robust ($2.27B operating cash flow).

#### Q4: Is the company positioned for growth and profitability?
Yes. With eight consecutive quarters of positive EPS expansion and forward P/E compressing to 28.77, the company is positioned to translate top-line stability into operating margin expansion as fixed-cost absorption improves.

### Industry Analysis: 3 Key Industry Questions

#### Q1: How is the company's industry doing as a whole?
The specialized analog and mixed-signal semiconductor industry is emerging from an extended inventory normalization cycle. Capital expenditures across automotive and industrial end-markets are stabilizing, supported by structural demand for automation and green energy transitions.
