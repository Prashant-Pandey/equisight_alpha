    AI-driven dynamic menu pricing algorithms, compounding multi-month sentiment

    pre-session positioning and incremental informational value.

    already heavily discounted negative turnaround narratives and margin

    mean-reversion outweighed headline anxiety.

    logistical operating expenses.

    **Morningstar Rating of ★★ (2 Stars, Medium Uncertainty)** and a **Narrow

    ample operational runway to absorb prospective legal costs or digital
