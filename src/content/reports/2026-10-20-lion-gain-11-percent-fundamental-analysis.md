    Legendary Entertainment and European media bidders could spark a competitive

    succeed in forcing a strategic asset carve-out or minority library

    remaining equity into a high-margin pure-play producer.

    - **Intellectual Property Scarcity:** In a streaming landscape starved for
    premium, proven film franchises (*The Hunger Games*, *John Wick*,

    #### Empirical Bear Case

    - **Valuation Multiple Reversion:** At $12.38, the equity trades 46.9% above
    its multi-model Consensus Fair Value ($8.43). In the event that exploratory
    acquisition discussions break down without a binding definitive agreement,
    the speculative M&A premium could rapidly evaporate, driving shares back
    toward standalone fair value.

    - **Restrictive Financing Hurdles:** With the US 10-Year yield at 5.12%,
    acquirers face high debt-service costs to absorb Lionsgate’s $1.90B debt

    final transaction terms.

    - **Standalone Operational Drag:** Fundamental performance remains
    encumbered by thin operating profitability (4.6% margin) and subdued Free
    Cash Flow ($0.10B), leaving little margin for error if TV studio weakness
